Clear both roots at once with $x = t^6$, so $\sqrt{x} = t^3$, $\sqrt[3]{x} = t^2$ and $dx = 6t^5 \, dt$:

$$
\int_0^1 \frac{6t^5}{t^3 + t^2} \, dt = 6 \int_0^1 \frac{t^3}{t + 1} \, dt.
$$

Long division gives $\frac{t^3}{t + 1} = t^2 - t + 1 - \frac{1}{t + 1}$, so the integral is

$$
6\left(\frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{2} + 1 - \ln 2\right) = 5 - 6\ln 2.
$$
